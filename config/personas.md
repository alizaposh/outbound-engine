# Config: Personas

Per-persona guidance for message generation. Outbound targets **Premium** and **Pro**
organizers who are currently on a competitor platform. This is also where stakeholder
feedback ("for this persona, lead with X, not Y") gets encoded as a reusable rule.

## How to identify which persona
Key off signals in the lead source / research:
- **Current platform** (the biggest tell, and the thing to displace):
  Eventbrite / Instagram / Plots / RA → likely **Premium**;
  Dice / Tixr / Shotgun / large-scale Eventbrite → likely **Pro**.
- **Event volume & attendance:** ~1–6 events/mo, 30–100 attendees → Premium;
  monthly/quarterly large-scale, 50+ to thousands, multi-city → Pro.
- **Ticket price:** ~$5–$100 → Premium; higher / festival pricing → Pro.
If unclear, default to Premium framing and flag for human review.

## Persona: Premium organizer
- **Typical profile:** mature organizers / small businesses; 1–6 events/month;
  30–100 attendees; tickets ~$5–$100; strong branding; lean teams (1–3 people).
- **Likely on now:** Eventbrite, Instagram, Plots, RA.
- **What they care about:** a platform that matches their premium aesthetic; less
  manual work (text blasts, follow-up campaigns); fast RSVPs, guest approval, simple
  payouts; growth data; responsive support.
- **Lead with:** the operational headache + brand mismatch of their current tool —
  Posh automates the manual marketing (text blasts, follow-ups) and matches a premium
  brand, with the marketplace bringing new attendees. The 40%-better-conversion and
  13%-marketplace stats land well here.
- **Avoid:** heavy enterprise / "scale to thousands" language — they're lean; don't
  overwhelm them.
- **Preferred CTA:** soft interest-check that invites a reply and leads toward a
  meeting. Example: "Open to exploring how Posh can help sell out your next event?
  Let me know if you'd like to connect." Keep the opener reply-based — no booking
  link yet; offer a specific time or scheduling link only once they show interest.

## Persona: Pro organizer
- **Typical profile:** high-revenue operators; monthly/quarterly festivals or large
  activations; 50+ to thousands of attendees; multi-city; mature teams; complex
  marketing/production; partner with sponsors/venues/vendors; **often already
  comparing ticketing platforms.**
- **Likely on now:** Dice, Tixr, Instagram, Eventbrite, Shotgun.
- **What they care about:** platform scalability/reliability; strategic partnership +
  high-touch sales/success; affiliate/ambassador tools for organic growth; deep
  analytics.
- **Lead with:** scale + partnership + organic growth. They're in evaluation mode, so
  pair the marketplace / organic-growth angle (13% of tickets via the marketplace)
  with the competitive offer (dedicated support, financial incentives, marketing push).
- **Avoid:** "easy, lightweight tool" framing — too basic; don't undersell the platform.
- **Preferred CTA:** still a connect-ask, but a touch more concrete since they're
  already comparing — offer a quick tailored look. Example: "Worth a short call to
  see how Posh stacks up for your next run of events? Happy to put together a
  tailored comparison." Reply-based first; scheduling link once interested.

## Not a cold-outbound target: Free tier
- Free-tier organizers (RSVP-only, $0 tickets, 1–50 attendees, often new to event
  tech) are typically already on Posh or very early. Handle as **product-led
  expansion / upsell**, not cold outbound. If one appears in a cold list, flag it
  rather than send.

## Default (no persona match)
- Use Premium framing, keep it brand- and ops-focused, and flag for human review so
  we can refine the persona later.
